BOOKAM Event Submission & Exclusive Ticketing Terms

Agreement Overview

This Event Submission & Exclusive Ticketing Terms Agreement governs the submission, hosting, ticketing, voting, and payment collection for all events listed on BOOKAM by registered organizers and event creators.

1. Exclusive Ticketing & Payment Platform

The Organizer hereby appoints BOOKAM as the exclusive authorized provider of online ticket sales, digital registrations, voting contests, and payment collections for the designated event(s). All digital passes, VIP packages, and general admission tickets must be routed and purchased via the official Bookam platform.

2. Prohibition of Duplicate / Third-Party Listings

To prevent ticket counterfeiting, entry disputes, and inventory synchronization errors, the Organizer agrees NOT to list, publish, or sell duplicate tickets for the same event on competing ticketing aggregators or unauthorized payment links during the active ticketing campaign.

3. Social Media & Promotional Bio Requirement

The Organizer agrees to prominently display and feature the official Bookam event link (bookam.ng/e/[event-id]) as the primary ticketing link in the official Instagram bio, Twitter/X bio, promotional posters, digital flyers, and marketing campaigns.

4. Gate Admission & QR Scanner Protocol

All attendee tickets issued by Bookam contain unique, cryptographically signed 2D QR codes. Entry verification at the venue gates must be conducted using the Bookam Organizer Check-in Scanner to prevent duplicate admissions and fraudulent entries.

5. Revenue Settlement & Direct Bank Payouts

Ticket sales proceeds and contestant voting revenues are recorded in real-time on the Organizer Dashboard and reconciled for settlement to the registered Nigerian bank account (e.g. Moniepoint Microfinance Bank). Settlements are processed according to the selected payout preference (Daily, Weekly, or Post-Event).

6. Influencer Commissions & Affiliate Tracking

When an Organizer configures tracked influencer promo codes or referral links, Bookam automatically computes attendee discounts and influencer commission splits from completed orders.

7. Host Integrity, Event Delivery & Attendee Safety

The Organizer guarantees that the event details, lineup, date, and venue are authentic, and undertakes full responsibility for event delivery, attendee security, and venue compliance.
