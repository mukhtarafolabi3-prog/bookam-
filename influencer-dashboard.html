First Time Here? How BOOKAM Promoter Commissions Work

Earn instant money promoting top events in Nigeria with 4 simple steps.

1

Get Your Code & Link

You receive a dedicated promo code (e.g. TUNDE10) and custom referral link giving attendees discounts on ticket checkouts.

2

Share With Audience

Share on WhatsApp, X (Twitter), TikTok bio, Instagram stories, or group chats. Every click is recorded automatically.

3

Earn On Every Ticket

Whenever a fan purchases a ticket using your link or code, your commission (e.g. 5% - 15%) is instantly credited in real-time.

4

Direct Bank Payout

The event host approves your sales ledger and settles your earnings directly to your bank account (Moniepoint, GTB, Zenith, etc.).
