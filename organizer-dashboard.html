BOOKAM EVENT SUBMISSION & EXCLUSIVE TICKETING TERMS

Official Platform Exclusivity & Settlement Agreement

By submitting an event to Bookam, the Event Organiser (“Organiser”) agrees to the following terms:

1. BOOKAM AS THE OFFICIAL TICKETING PLATFORM

The Organiser agrees that Bookam will be the official and exclusive ticketing platform for the submitted event.

The Organiser shall not use, promote, publish, or authorise another ticketing platform to sell tickets for the same event without prior written approval from Bookam.

2. PAYMENT COLLECTION

All online ticket payments for the event shall be processed through Bookam.

The Organiser shall not intentionally redirect attendees to another payment or ticketing platform for the purchase of event tickets.

3. EVENT PROMOTION

The Organiser agrees to use the official Bookam ticket link provided for the event when promoting ticket sales online, including on social media, websites, advertisements and promotional materials.

Where applicable, Bookam may provide referral links, influencer links or promo codes for tracking ticket sales.

4. EVENT INFORMATION

The Organiser confirms that all information submitted to Bookam, including the event name, date, venue, ticket prices, ticket categories, event description and promotional materials, is accurate and that the Organiser has the necessary authority to organise and sell tickets for the event.

5. BOOKAM SERVICES

Bookam may provide ticket creation, payment processing, digital tickets, QR-code verification, attendee management, sales analytics, promotional tools and technical support as applicable.

6. BREACH

If the Organiser breaches these terms by using another ticketing or payment platform for the event without Bookam's approval, Bookam may suspend or remove the event from the platform and take appropriate action regarding outstanding fees, payments, refunds or other obligations.

7. ACCEPTANCE

By clicking “I Agree & Submit Event”, the Organiser confirms that they have read, understood and accepted these Event Submission & Exclusive Ticketing Terms.
